About

The Photographer Behind Amour Affairs

Taher Husain is the founder of Amour Affairs, a wedding photography and cinematography studio based in Pune, India.

His approach to wedding photography focuses on observation, timing and authentic moments. He focuses on documenting the emotions, relationships and details that naturally unfold during a wedding celebration.

Through Amour Affairs, Taher Husain and his team document weddings with an emphasis on candid storytelling, genuine emotions and photographs that preserve the atmosphere of each celebration.
