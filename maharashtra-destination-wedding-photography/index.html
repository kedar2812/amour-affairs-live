The Maharashtra Wedding Escape: Seven Beautiful Destinations to Say “I Do”

Amour Affairs 8 min read 12 October 2026

What if your wedding could be more than just a celebration?

What if the location itself became part of your story?

A misty morning in the hills. A quiet couple portrait surrounded by greenery. A celebration overlooking the coast. Or a vibrant city wedding filled with energy, family and tradition.

Maharashtra gives couples all of these possibilities.

From the familiar charm of Pune to the hills of Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani, the scenic beauty of Lonavala and Khandala, the coastline of Alibaug and the unmistakable character of Mumbai, there are plenty of destinations to consider when planning a wedding or couple shoot.

And when the location matters, your photography should feel connected to it too.

At Amour Affairs, a Pune-based wedding photography and cinematography studio, the approach is simple: capture the wedding as it actually feels — the people, emotions, little details and moments that you may not notice while they are happening.

So, which Maharashtra destination feels most like your story?

The seven destinations

  1. Mahabaleshwar
  2. Panchgani
  3. Mumbai
  4. Lonavala
  5. Khandala
  6. Alibaug
  7. Pune

1. Mahabaleshwar — For the Couple Who Loves the Hills

Imagine waking up to cool mountain air, surrounded by greenery, and beginning your wedding day somewhere that already feels like an escape.

That is the charm of Mahabaleshwar.

The hill station offers a naturally romantic setting for destination weddings, intimate celebrations and couple portraits. The changing landscapes can give your wedding gallery a beautiful sense of variety without making the photographs feel overly staged.

Think quiet portraits, candid conversations, family photographs and those little moments between ceremonies.

If you’re planning a wedding here, think beyond the main ceremony. Give your photography team enough time to capture the atmosphere of the destination as well as the wedding itself.

You can also explore the Amour Affairs wedding portfolio to see how full celebrations are documented.

2. Panchgani — A Little More Intimate, A Lot More Scenic

Looking for something quieter?

Panchgani offers a more peaceful hill-station atmosphere and can be a beautiful choice for couples who want their wedding photographs to feel intimate and natural.

The surrounding landscapes can become part of your story rather than simply acting as a backdrop.

  • A couple walking together.
  • A quiet conversation before the ceremony.
  • Family members enjoying the celebration.
  • A moment of laughter that nobody planned.

These are often the photographs that make a wedding album feel personal.

For a Panchgani celebration, discuss your venue, wedding schedule and preferred outdoor locations with your photographer before the wedding so there is enough time to explore the setting.

3. Mumbai — For the City-Loving Couple

Not every love story belongs in the hills.

Some belong in the middle of the city.

Mumbai brings its own personality to wedding photography — energetic, modern, glamorous and constantly moving.

Whether your celebration takes place in an elegant indoor venue or you want a contemporary couple shoot around the city, Mumbai gives you plenty of visual possibilities.

The key here is planning.

Traffic, venue timings and travel between locations can all affect your photography schedule. A little planning beforehand means you can spend less time worrying about logistics and more time actually enjoying your day.

To see how Amour Affairs approaches portraits away from the wedding day, browse the couple-shoot collection.

4. Lonavala — Where the Journey Becomes Part of the Story

Lonavala has long been a favourite escape from Pune and Mumbai — and it is easy to understand why.

Green landscapes, hills and a relaxed atmosphere make it a natural choice for couples looking for something scenic without travelling too far.

It can work beautifully for a destination celebration, engagement session or pre-wedding shoot.

And here’s something worth remembering:

Don’t plan your photographs around the location alone. Plan them around how you want to feel.

  • If you want relaxed photographs, leave enough breathing room in your schedule.
  • If you want dramatic portraits, discuss the location and timing beforehand.
  • If you want candid photographs, give your photographer the freedom to observe rather than constantly pose you.

Planning a pre-wedding session here? Our couple shoots page shows the kind of relaxed, natural portraits we aim for.

5. Khandala — For Romantic Hill-Top Moments

Just a short distance from Lonavala, Khandala offers another beautiful hill setting for couples who want their photographs to have a natural, romantic atmosphere.

The landscape can work especially well for couple portraits and intimate moments away from the main celebration.

But don’t feel like every photograph needs to be a grand landscape shot.

Sometimes the best photograph is simply the two of you talking while everyone else is getting ready.

Sometimes it’s your parents watching you.

Sometimes it’s a quiet moment before you walk into the ceremony.

That’s what makes wedding photography different from simply taking beautiful pictures.

It’s about remembering how the day felt.

6. Alibaug — When the Coast Becomes Part of Your Wedding

Now let’s leave the hills behind.

If your idea of a perfect celebration involves open skies, coastal surroundings and a more relaxed atmosphere, Alibaug could be the destination for you.

Beachside celebrations and elegant venues create plenty of opportunities for natural couple portraits, candid photographs and photographs that feel completely different from a traditional city wedding.

The biggest advantage?

The destination itself gives your wedding story another layer.

Your photographs don’t just say, this was our wedding.

They also say, this was where our story happened.

Each celebration in the Amour Affairs wedding collection is told as a complete story, from the first ritual to the last dance.

7. Pune — Where Tradition Meets Contemporary Celebrations

And then there is Pune.

For Amour Affairs, Pune isn’t just another destination. It is home.

The city offers an incredible variety of wedding celebrations — from intimate gatherings to large multi-function weddings, traditional ceremonies and contemporary celebrations.

It also gives couples flexibility when planning photography, with a wide range of venues and settings available within and around the city. If you’re still choosing one, our guide to wedding photography at Pune venues is a good place to start.

Most importantly, Pune allows the photography team to focus on what matters most: the people.

At Amour Affairs, the team describes its approach as storytelling driven by real moments, emotions and the individual story of each couple.

Which Maharashtra Destination Feels Like You?

Let’s make this simple.

  • Want hills and greenery? Mahabaleshwar or Panchgani.
  • Want a scenic escape close to the city? Lonavala or Khandala.
  • Want the coast? Alibaug.
  • Want a vibrant urban celebration? Mumbai.
  • Want variety, convenience and countless wedding possibilities? Pune.

But here’s the more important question:

Where can you imagine yourself looking back at your wedding photographs 20 years from now and still feeling exactly what you felt that day?

That’s the destination worth choosing.

Planning Your Maharashtra Wedding Photography?

Once your destination is decided, the next step is making sure your photography plan works with it.

Give your photographer the full schedule

Don’t just share the ceremony time.

Share the complete wedding timeline, including getting ready, rituals, family photographs, couple portraits and travel between venues.

Leave room for real moments

Some of the best photographs happen between planned events.

Don’t fill every minute of your schedule. Give yourself time to breathe, enjoy your family and actually experience the wedding.

Tell your photographer what matters

  • Are there grandparents who must be photographed?
  • A special family tradition?
  • A particular ritual?
  • A friend who has travelled from another country?

Tell your photographer. The more they understand your story, the better they can document it.

Think beyond the wedding day

Your story doesn’t necessarily have to begin with the first ceremony.

A couple shoot or pre-wedding session can give you a completely different set of photographs — relaxed, personal and away from the pressure of the wedding day.

Choose a team you feel comfortable with

You are going to spend a significant part of your wedding day around your photography team. So ask yourself:

  • Do we feel comfortable around them?
  • Do we like the way they tell stories?
  • Do their photographs feel natural to us?
  • Can we trust them to notice the moments we might miss?
