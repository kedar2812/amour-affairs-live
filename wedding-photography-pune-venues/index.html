Pune Wedding Venues

Wedding Photography at The Orchid Hotel Pune, Oxford Golf & The Corinthians

Amour Affairs 8 min read 25 September 2026

A wedding venue becomes much more than a location on the day of the celebration. It becomes the backdrop to the moments couples remember for years — the first look, a parent’s reaction, laughter with friends, emotional ceremonies and the quiet moments shared between the bride and groom.

Pune offers couples a wide variety of wedding settings, from elegant hotels and spacious venues to open landscapes and resort-style celebrations. With so many options available, choosing a venue often comes down to the kind of experience you want to create and the atmosphere you want your wedding photographs to have.

For couples planning a wedding in Pune, The Orchid Hotel Pune in Balewadi, Oxford Golf Pune and The Corinthians are three venues worth considering.

Each venue has its own character, but regardless of where you celebrate, great wedding photography is not simply about photographing the venue. It is about using the surroundings while keeping the focus on the people, emotions and moments that make your wedding yours.

Wedding Photography at The Orchid Hotel Pune, Balewadi

The Orchid Hotel Pune in Balewadi can be a convenient option for couples looking for a hotel wedding in Pune.

When planning photography at a hotel venue, couples can think beyond the main wedding ceremony. The wedding story can begin with the getting-ready photographs, family interactions and small details before the celebrations officially begin.

Photographs of the wedding outfits, jewellery, invitations, décor and other details can help tell the story of the day from beginning to end.

Once the celebrations begin, the focus naturally moves towards the people. Family photographs, candid guest interactions, emotional moments during the ceremony and couple portraits can all become part of the final wedding collection.

One useful thing for couples to discuss with their photographer is the photography timeline.

Wedding days can become busy very quickly, particularly when multiple functions are taking place. Setting aside a little time for family photographs and couple portraits can make it easier to create relaxed photographs without rushing through the rest of the celebration.

The best photographs are also not always the ones where everyone is looking directly at the camera.

A parent watching their child during the ceremony, friends laughing together, siblings helping the bride or groom get ready, or a couple sharing a quiet moment can often become some of the most meaningful photographs from the entire wedding.

If you’re planning your wedding at The Orchid Hotel Pune, discussing the schedule with your photographer beforehand can help ensure that these moments are documented naturally.

Wedding Photography at Oxford Golf Pune

For couples who want their wedding photography to include open surroundings and a more natural atmosphere, Oxford Golf Pune offers a different setting from a traditional hotel wedding.

The surroundings can become an important part of the visual story. Instead of photographing every portrait against the same background, couples can discuss ways of incorporating the venue’s outdoor environment into their photographs.

This can be especially useful for couple portraits.

A wedding photographer can look for opportunities to create a mixture of wide photographs that show the environment and closer photographs that focus on the couple and their emotions.

Timing can also play an important role.

Natural light changes throughout the day, so couples planning outdoor portraits may want to discuss the ideal time for photographs with their photography team. A short portrait session around suitable natural light can create a completely different feeling from photographs taken in the middle of a busy ceremony schedule.

However, wedding photography should not only focus on portraits.

The real story is happening around the couple — family members arriving, friends meeting after a long time, conversations between ceremonies and all the spontaneous moments that happen when people are simply enjoying themselves.

This is where candid photography can complement planned portraits.

If you’re considering Oxford Golf Pune for your wedding, think about how you want the surrounding environment to appear in your photographs while still keeping the people and emotions at the centre of the story.

Wedding Photography at The Corinthians

The Corinthians offers couples a resort-style setting for their wedding celebrations.

For photography, a setting like this can allow couples to think about their wedding as a complete experience rather than only focusing on the main ceremony.

The story can begin with preparations and continue through the celebrations, ceremonies, family photographs, couple portraits and candid moments with guests.

One of the most important things to remember is that you don’t have to photograph everything in exactly the same way.

Some moments deserve a traditional approach — such as important family photographs or certain ceremonial moments.

Other moments are better captured naturally.

A grandmother smiling during a ceremony, friends reacting to a speech, siblings laughing together or the couple taking a quiet break from the celebrations can all become important parts of the final gallery.

For couples planning a wedding at The Corinthians, it can be helpful to share your wedding schedule and priorities with your photographer beforehand.

If there are particular family members, traditions or moments that are especially important to you, mentioning them in advance gives the photography team a better understanding of what matters most to you.

How to Get Better Wedding Photographs at Your Pune Venue

Your venue is only one part of creating beautiful wedding photographs.

The way your day is planned can make an equally important difference.

Before your wedding, consider discussing the following with your photographer:

  • Plan enough time for portraits.
  • Share your important moments.
  • Don’t plan every photograph.
  • Think about natural light.
  • Look at complete wedding galleries.

A full wedding gallery shows how the photographer handles different parts of a wedding — from getting ready and family photographs to ceremonies, portraits, candid moments and the reception.

Choosing Your Wedding Photography Style

Every couple has a different idea of what their wedding photographs should feel like.

Some couples prefer timeless and traditional portraits. Others want photographs that feel candid, natural and documentary-style.

Many couples choose a combination of both.

Traditional photographs ensure that important family and ceremonial moments are documented, while candid photography captures the emotions and interactions happening naturally throughout the day.

When choosing a photographer, think about the photographs you would actually want to look at years from now.

Planning Your Wedding Photography in Pune

Once your venue is finalised, it is worth discussing photography early in the planning process.

Before booking your photographer, discuss:

  • Number of wedding functions
  • Expected guest count
  • Coverage hours
  • Number of photographers required
  • Photography and cinematography requirements
  • Albums and other deliverables
  • Wedding film requirements
  • Delivery timeline
  • Important family members and ceremonies
  • Any specific photographs you want captured

It is also useful to coordinate the photography timeline with your wedding planner and other vendors.

Which Pune Wedding Venue Is Right for Your Wedding?

There is no single venue that is right for every couple.

The right choice depends on your guest list, wedding style, number of functions, budget, location preferences and the kind of experience you want to create.

The Orchid Hotel Pune, Balewadi can suit couples looking for a hotel-based wedding setting.

Oxford Golf Pune can appeal to couples who want their wedding photographs to incorporate open surroundings and a more natural environment.

The Corinthians offers a resort-style setting for couples planning a wedding celebration with a distinctive atmosphere.

Whichever venue you choose, remember that the venue is only the backdrop.

The people, emotions and relationships are what make the photographs meaningful.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I look for in a wedding photographer in Pune?

Look at complete wedding galleries, understand the photographer’s style and check what is included in their package. It is also important to speak with the photographer before booking to make sure their approach feels right for you.

How early should I book a wedding photographer?

It is better to start researching photographers well before the wedding, particularly if your date falls during a busy wedding season.

Is candid photography suitable for large weddings?

Yes. Candid photography can work particularly well for large celebrations because it allows the photographer to document natural interactions between the couple, families and guests.

Should we plan time for couple portraits?

Yes. Even a short dedicated window can give you an opportunity to create relaxed couple portraits without taking too much time away from the celebrations.

What should we discuss with our photographer before the wedding?

Discuss the wedding schedule, functions, coverage hours, important family members, photography style, deliverables and any specific moments you want documented.

Can our wedding photographer capture both candid and traditional photographs?

Yes. Many couples choose a combination of candid photography and traditional portraits so that important family photographs and ceremonies are documented alongside natural moments.
